## Risks and notes

- Duplicate titles remain by decision: 15 title texts across 30 pages (core service page and its `/las-vegas/` twin, plus the `/plumbing-services/` and `/las-vegas-plumbing-services/` pair). Differentiation is a separate task.
- 79 titles have no brand suffix, as approved.
- Green Valley and Lake Las Vegas titles drop ", Henderson", as approved. Their H1s and `Service.name` still say "Henderson", because J3 was rejected.
- Aliante titles on five pages read "…in Aliante, North Las Vegas" with no ", NV" and no suffix; the Aliante water heater page keeps "…in Aliante, NV". Aliante H1s were not changed.
- The breadcrumb `ListItem.name` still holds the old wording on 4 pages (two Aliante, North Las Vegas garbage disposal, North Las Vegas repiping), matching their visible breadcrumbs. It no longer matches the new page titles; that is accepted.
- `docs/seo/route-manifest.json` has uncommitted regenerated changes (titles only). It was not on the 132-file scope.
- Descriptions still carry "4.8-star rated" (7 core service pages) and "over 40 years" (About), owner-approved.
- Google may take time to recrawl; search snippets will not change immediately.
