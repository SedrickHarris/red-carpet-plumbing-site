# Launch Blockers: Gas-Leak Safety Copy and Homepage Hours Schema

Branch main at a280deb plus the edits described below. Written 2026-10-12.

## Summary

- Blocker A: gas-leak call instructions now say to call 911 and Southwest Gas. The old numbers 1-800-935-4748 and 1-800-654-2765 are gone. The number 877-860-6020 replaces them, only where an old number appeared.
- Blocker B: the homepage no longer emits `openingHoursSpecification`, because the hours are not in its visible text. The 11 hubs, the Aliante hub, Contact and About keep the block because their hours are visible. The audit premise that the hubs did not show hours was wrong: every hub renders "Office hours are Monday through Friday, 7:30 AM to 4:30 PM. Emergency plumbing service is available 24/7."
- 77 source lines changed in 21 gas-copy files, plus the homepage block removal (8 lines replaced by 1 comment line). One generated file, `docs/seo/route-manifest.json`, also changed because the build regenerates it.
